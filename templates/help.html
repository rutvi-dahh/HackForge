Step-by-Step Reimbursement Guide

1. 15-Day Submission Window

Receipts must be uploaded within 15 days of their printed issue date. Day 15 is valid. Receipts uploaded on day 16 or later are automatically rejected by the policy engine without exception.

2. Required Receipt Data

Our AI model scans documents for: Issue Date, Total Amount & Currency, and Unique Invoice/Bill Number. If any of these fields are illegible or low-confidence, your claim status becomes "Info needed". You will simply fill in the missing fields to re-evaluate the claim without penalty.

3. Mandatory Employee Inputs

  • Category & Context: Select from Food, Travel, Accommodation, Equipment, or Miscellaneous; and specify Trip or Office context.
  • Attendee Count: Mandatory for all Food claims (must be an integer ≥ 1). Meal limits are calculated per person.
  • Client Name: Required if the expense is flagged as client-related.

4. Strictly Prohibited Goods (Auto-Rejection)

Alcohol, tobacco, fines/penalties, personal clothing/care items, personal gifts, and gambling are strictly non-reimbursable. If a receipt contains any prohibited item, the entire receipt is rejected whole. Mixed receipts cannot be split.

5. Spending Limits Flagging

Exceeding a spending limit (per-receipt, daily, or monthly category allowance) NEVER automatically rejects your claim. Instead, the claim is Flagged and forwarded to your administrator with the exact mathematical calculation so they can evaluate the business necessity.

6. Clarification Query on Rejections

If your receipt is auto-rejected, you cannot resubmit the file (it will be caught as a duplicate). However, you have the right to submit exactly one clarification query directly to the finance admin. If the admin overturns the rejection, your receipt immediately becomes Approved.
